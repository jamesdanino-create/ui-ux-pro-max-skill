Our Story

A Local Business with a Lasting Vision

Greenarc was founded with a simple belief: great landscaping does more than just look good — it creates healthier environments, stronger communities, and a better quality of life.

As a locally owned and operated company, we take pride in our work, our customers and our community. We’re passionate about bringing outdoor spaces to life, one project at a time.
