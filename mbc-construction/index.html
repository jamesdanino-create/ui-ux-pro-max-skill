Our Company

A niche for heritage, a name for reliability.

MBC Construction Limited was established in March 2013 by owners Moy Benzaquen and Elias Attias, who spotted a niche in the market at the time: the refurbishment of old residential buildings in Gibraltar. The company has since grown to take on a wide range of low-to-medium sized new build, refurbishment, upkeep and demolition projects.

MBC is well known locally for its organisation, moving projects forward in a timely fashion, within a client's budget, and with extreme care to final details. The team treats every job — big or small — as "a work of art," aiming to complete each contract smoothly and with the least headache for the client.

Specialising in epoxy resin finishes and a wide range of trades, MBC keeps projects entirely in-house without subcontractors, and operates its own yard and storage facility at 16 Catalan Bay Road for processing and distributing building materials.

Recognised for heritage craftsmanship

On three occasions, clients of MBC have received the Gibraltar Heritage Award for the refurbishment of old, derelict buildings — brought back to life while keeping their historical details intact.
